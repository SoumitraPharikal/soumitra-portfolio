## Project Overview

Conducted under the mentorship of **Dr. Vyas Akondi** at IISER Berhampur, investigating the analytical transformation of optical wavefront aberrations when pupil stops change dimension.

### Key Contributions
- Implemented Zernike circle polynomial expansions $W(\rho, \theta) = \sum_{n,m} c_n^m Z_n^m(\rho, \theta)$ to model optical aberrations across continuously scaled circular apertures.
- Derived and evaluated conversion matrices $\mathbf{T}(s)$ in MATLAB to account for modal cross-coupling when reducing pupil radius $s = R_2 / R_1 \le 1$.
- Quantified Root-Mean-Square (RMS) wavefront phase errors and Maréchal Strehl ratio degradation for applications in ophthalmic diagnostic wavefront sensors and high-resolution microscopy.
