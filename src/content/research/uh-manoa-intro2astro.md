## Programme Overview

Participated in **Intro2Astro**, an intensive 8-week research training program organized by the University of Hawaii at Manoa and directed by **Dr. Fei Dai**, emphasizing modern observational astronomy, space-based transit data pipelines, and exoplanet detection methodologies.

### Technical & Scientific Scope
- Extracted and reduced high-precision space-borne photometric time-series from the Transiting Exoplanet Survey Satellite (**TESS**), the **Gaia DR3** astrometric catalogue, and the **MAST** archive.
- Engineered automated Python pipelines utilizing `Lightkurve` and `Astropy` for systematics correction, cotrending basis vectors, and stellar variability detrending.
- Executed phase-folding algorithms and Box Least Squares (BLS) transit modeling to detect candidate exoplanetary transits and constrain planet radii and orbital periods.
- Concurrently participated remotely in the **2025 Caltech Sagan Summer Workshop**, focusing on advanced exoplanet imaging and characterization techniques.
