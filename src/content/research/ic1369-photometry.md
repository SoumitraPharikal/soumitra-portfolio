## Project Overview

Conducted at the Indian Centre for Space Physics (ICSP), Kolkata under the supervision of **Dr. Devendra Bisht**, this observational astrophysics investigation examined the variable star population and fundamental physical properties of the Galactic open cluster **IC 1369**.

### Key Technical Contributions
- Reduced and calibrated **814 time-series CCD image frames** acquired across multi-band optical filters ($U, B, V, R, I$).
- Executed high-precision Point Spread Function (PSF) photometry using `DAOPHOT` and aperture curve-of-growth optimization in `photutils`.
- Performed **Lomb-Scargle periodogram analysis** and phase-folding to isolate and characterize **four candidate variable stars** within the cluster field, resolving periodicities and light curve amplitudes.
- Constructed standard Color-Magnitude Diagrams ($V \text{ vs. } B-V$ and $V \text{ vs. } V-I$) and derived cluster age, distance modulus, and reddening through theoretical Padova/PARSEC isochrone fitting.
