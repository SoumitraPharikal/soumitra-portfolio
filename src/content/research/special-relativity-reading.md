## Project Overview

A structured theoretical reading project conducted under the guidance of **Dr. Sandeep Chatterjee** at IISER Berhampur, systematically deriving the kinematics and dynamics of relativistic mechanics in flat spacetime.

### Topics & Mathematical Derivations
- Einstein's postulates of special relativity and constancy of the invariant speed $c$.
- Geometric structure of Minkowski spacetime $(\mathbb{R}^{1,3}, \eta_{\mu\nu})$ with metric signature $(+,-,-,-)$.
- Algebraic derivation of Lorentz coordinate boosts and consequences: relativity of simultaneity, kinematic time dilation, and FitzGerald-Lorentz contraction.
- Four-vector formulation: four-velocity $U^\mu = \gamma(c, \mathbf{u})$, four-momentum $P^\mu = m_0 U^\mu$, and mass-shell invariant $P^\mu P_\mu = m_0^2 c^2$.
- Introductory tensorial notation and covariant formulation of relativistic conservation laws.
