## Representative Mathematical Formulation

### Fermi's Golden Rule & Born Scattering Amplitude

$$\Gamma_{i \to f} = \frac{2\pi}{\hbar} \left| \langle f | \hat{V} | i \rangle \right|^2 \rho(E_f), \quad f(\theta, \phi) = -\frac{m}{2\pi \hbar^2} \int e^{-i\mathbf{q}\cdot\mathbf{r}'} V(\mathbf{r}') d^3\mathbf{r}'$$
