## Representative Mathematical Formulation

### Sturm-Liouville Eigenvalue Operator & Green's Function

$$\mathcal{L} u(x) = -\frac{d}{dx}\left[p(x)\frac{du}{dx}\right] + q(x)u = \lambda w(x)u, \quad G(x, \xi) = \sum_n \frac{u_n(x)u_n(\xi)}{\lambda_n}$$
