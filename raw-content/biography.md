## 3. Academic Profile & Statement of Intent

Soumitra's primary research work engages both observational astronomy and theoretical/computational optical physics. In observational astronomy, his work centers on precision CCD data reduction, aperture and PSF photometry, stellar light-curve extraction, and stellar cluster parameter determination (including PARSEC isochrone fitting for open clusters such as IC 1369). In optical and wave physics, his investigations focus on modal decomposition of optical wavefront aberrations using orthogonal Zernike polynomials over continuous and scaled circular apertures, analyzing modal cross-coupling and wavefront phase variance.

In parallel with his physical research, he applies modern computational systems languages—notably **Rust**, **Python**, and **C/C++**—to build deterministic scientific tooling, astrodynamics propagation engines, and data pipelines that adhere to high numerical stability and reproducible standards.
